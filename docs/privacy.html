Game data stored locally

The game stores progress, achievements, equipment choices and settings in local files on the device. The game does not provide an account, cloud save, analytics service or Play Games sign-in. Android backup and device transfer are disabled for the game's local files. Uninstalling the app may remove local progress.

Advertising and consent services

The Android app includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Advertising is enabled in test builds and can be enabled in a release only through an explicit production configuration. When advertising is enabled and UMP permits ad requests, Google may process IP addresses, device or account identifiers, product interactions, advertising data and diagnostic information. An IP address may be used to estimate general location. The game does not request precise location permission.

UMP may use network access and local storage to request consent information, display privacy messages and remember privacy choices. Where Google reports that privacy options are required, the game provides a Privacy Options entry in Settings. Changing a privacy choice invalidates ads already loaded by the game.

Rewarded ads are optional. An occasional interstitial may be configured at a natural transition after successful play. Core gameplay and local progress do not require an internet connection. Advertising and consent services require network access when used.

Other device features

Optional haptic feedback uses the device vibration feature and does not collect sensor data. The app does not include in-app billing.

Website privacy

These static support pages contain no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, web forms or cookies set by the site code. Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary request data under the hosting provider's own terms.

Your choices and contact

You can adjust available advertising privacy choices through the in-game Privacy Options entry when UMP requires it. For privacy or support questions, email ykucukcinar@gmail.com.
